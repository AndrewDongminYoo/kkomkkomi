# Plans

Moved unchanged from `CLAUDE.md` § Architecture on 2026-11-02.

- **Plans.** `PlansCubit` in `lib/presentation/plans/` reads the plan, and the offers and the management page when `Entitlements.sellsPlans` is true, and follows `planChanges`. A purchase never sets the plan: only `currentPlan()` and `planChanges` do, and after a purchase, a restore, or a reported change it reads the offers again, because a switch of the period keeps the plan and the store reports no change. The store subscription management changes the subscription outside the app, and `launchUrl` answers when the store page opens, so `PlansView` calls `PlansCubit.refresh()` through an `AppLifecycleListener` each time that the app comes back to the foreground: it calls `Entitlements.invalidate()`, which makes `RevenueCatEntitlements` call `Purchases.invalidateCustomerInfoCache()`, because `getCustomerInfo` normally gives the customer information that RevenueCat keeps, and then reads the plan and the offers again. It does nothing before the plan loaded, in a build that does not sell plans, or while an action is on its way, and a plan that the store reports during its read replaces the answer of the read. A cancelled purchase shows nothing, a pending one says that it waits for approval until the store reports a new plan or a read of the offers marks the offer of that purchase as active, which a switch of the period needs because it keeps the plan, and a failure says that it did not work; a restore says what it found. `PlansState.pendingOfferId` records that offer while the message shows, and every change of the message drops it, so a failed read never ends the message and a later read never acts on an old record. `Entitlements.managementUrl()` answers a `ManagementLink`: a known answer replaces the management page, also with none, which the store gives after an expiry, and `ManagementLink.unknown()`, which `RevenueCatEntitlements` gives without a user ID or when the read fails, keeps the page from before. The screen takes no touch and no back press while a purchase or a restore is on its way, and after a purchase or a restore that found a plan until the offers are read again; a link that does not open while an action is on its way leaves the screen without touch. Only the read of the offers that started last changes the state, and an older read that answers later answers with the newer one. When the read after an action gives no offers, no offer stays marked, so both periods of the current plan count as in use. A failed read of the products in use leaves the offers unmarked, and does not fail the offers. `PlansState.isInUse` marks the product of the current plan that the store named as active, or both periods of the current plan when the store named none, and its control buys nothing, so a second purchase of the product in use never reaches the store. A build that does not sell plans shows the plan and the links, with no offer, no restore, and no purchase control. An empty answer of the offers shows a retry beside restore and the links. Each paid plan shows one control per period, with the `priceString` of the store, and the client limit and the footer of each plan come from `Plan.clientLimit` and `Plan.showsFooter`. The company profile screen opens `PlansPage.route()` and reads no plan. The `ExternalLinks` port opens a URL and answers whether it opened; `UrlLauncherExternalLinks` calls `launchUrl` with `LaunchMode.externalApplication` and never asks `canLaunchUrl`, which the `url_launcher` README says can answer false when `launchUrl` would work. It is the one file under `lib/` that imports `url_launcher`, and `test/presentation/url_launcher_import_test.dart` fails when a second file imports it.
